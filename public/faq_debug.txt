    [ckeditor] => <h2>Materials for Food Packaging</h2>

<p>Custom food boxes are manufactured using special materials. These materials have specific features to protect the food and keep it toxin free. Therefore, we use premium materials that are suitable for every type of food product. We are dealing in:</p>

<h3>Food-Grade Paperboard</h3>

<p>Food-grade paperboard is a foldable food compatible material. It is light-weight and comes in different thickness variations. It is completely safe for customized food boxes. Mostly it is used for bakery cartons, <a href="https://www.premiumboxes.com/cereal-boxes/">cereal boxes</a>, <a href="https://www.premiumboxes.com/snack-boxes/">snack packaging</a>, confectionery boxes, food sleeves, and lightweight takeout products.</p>

<h3>Kraft Paper</h3>

<p>Kraft paper has a natural appearance and is safe to use for food wrapping. It comes in brown colour and is suitable for products such as bakery products, takeaway cartons, food trays and sleeves. This material offers great resistance against tearing.</p>

<h3>Corrugated Board</h3>

<p><a href="https://www.premiumboxes.com/corrugated-boxes/">Corrugated board</a> is a thick material and has different variations. It is best known for its rigidity and strength. It is used for products which need extra care. Commonly it is used for <a href="https://www.premiumboxes.com/pizza-boxes/">pizza boxes</a>, catering orders, delivery packaging, subscription meals, and outer shipping cartons. Businesses can choose from different flutes available in corrugated board.</p>

<h3>Grease-Resistant Materials</h3>

<p>Different food items need different type of protection and packaging. Fried foot items need packaging that offers grease resistance to prevent the oil leakage. We use grease-resistant material for such products. These boxes are wrapped with special paper that offers protection against oil and sauce spillage.</p>

<h2>Match the Box to the Food</h2>

<p>Matching the box with food is quite important for any business. You cannot use just any box to pack you food items. Every food item requires different type of protection. Some items are hot, some are cold, some are oily and some are liquid. Brands have to use customized food boxes.</p>

<h3>Greasy and Fried Foods</h3>

<p>Greasy and fried foods like burgers, pizza, fries, and pastries need grease-resistant packaging. On the other hand, these boxes also offer ventilation so the food doesn&rsquo;t get soggy. No need to worry; at Premiumboxes we have product-specific packaging materials.</p>

<h3>Baked Goods and Desserts</h3>

<p>Bakery items need special packaging that offers protection and doesn&rsquo;t damage the presentation. Products such as doughnuts, cakes, pastries etc have special toppings. So the box must offer enough safe for the protection of icing, glazing, shape and toppings. Therefore, before getting the box look carefully to the box size and product size.</p>

<h3>Chilled and Frozen Foods</h3>

<p>Frozen food items need special packaging that offers water resistance. Get custom food packaging boxes from Premiumboxes that are manufactured specially for frozen food. These boxes will have resistance against condensation, barcode readability and strong closure.</p>

<h3>Dry and Shelf-Stable Foods</h3>

<p>Dry food products that are placed on shelves need entirely different packaging. For these products preferable packaging is space saving, sealed and shelf compatible.</p>
